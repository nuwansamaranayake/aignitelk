# Award section: Digital Innovation Impact Pioneer

Date: 2026-10-05
Surface: aignitelk.com home page (new first section), meta description, OpenGraph/Twitter image, Organization JSON-LD.
Copy source: Nuwan's CC task brief, used verbatim.

## 1. Diagnosis

The award is the strongest third-party proof on the site. Until now every trust signal on the page was self-described ("Silicon Valley engineering standards"). A named award at a named event, with a photo, is specific, dated and checkable. Agents can lift it as a fact. People remember it.

Main risk: association drift. Summaries can flatten "a programme organised by the ITC, a joint UN/WTO agency" into "a UN award". The copy is worded to prevent that, and it must stay that way.

## 2. Truth layer

- Award name: Digital Innovation Impact Pioneer.
- Recipient: AiGNITE Sri Lanka (the company), for the GoviHub platform.
- Venue: International Youth OPC Co-Creation Dialogue, Global Digital Trade Expo (GDTE), Hangzhou, China, September 2026.
- Organisers: International Trade Centre (ITC, joint agency of the UN and WTO), Global SDGs and Leadership Development Center, China International Youth Exchange Center.
- Accepted by: Aruni Samaranayake, Director of Operations.
- Cohort: 33 enterprises, 22 developing countries, 1 Sri Lankan company.

## 4. AI-washing and association risk register

- **UN association (high):** never say the UN gave the award. No UN, ITC, WTO or GDTE logos (UN emblem use needs permission). Text mentions only.
- **Number creep (medium):** no new statistics, quotes or rankings beyond the 3 numbers in the brief.
- **Entity naming (low):** the award names "AiGNITE Sri Lanka". The rest of the site uses "AiGNITE Software (Pvt) Ltd". Both refer to the same company. Fold this into the canonical-name decision in `brand-refresh-lk-theme.md`.

## 5. Human memory layer

- One image does the work: the trophy in hand at the expo.
- Short hook: "Digital Innovation Impact Pioneer". "1 Sri Lankan company in the cohort" is the most memorable number.

## 6. Agent surface plan

- The headline is the page's single `h1`. The old hero heading becomes an `h2`, with its content kept.
- Organization JSON-LD `award`: "Digital Innovation Impact Pioneer, Global Digital Trade Expo, Hangzhou, September 2026".
- The meta description gains one short award sentence.
- The OG/Twitter image is the 1200px award photo (JPG for broad crawler support).
- Descriptive alt text, copied verbatim from the brief.

2. A short news or press page for the award with the full story and photo credit. Impact medium, effort low.
3. Canonical company name across the site and JSON-LD. Impact medium, effort low.
